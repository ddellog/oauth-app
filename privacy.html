Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-02

This Privacy Policy describes how the "Personal Google API Utility" ("the App") handles data. The App is a private tool operated by a single individual ("the Owner") for the Owner's personal use only.

1. Who operates the App

The App is operated by an individual for personal purposes. Contact: pencil2535@mail.ru.

2. What data the App accesses

When the Owner runs the App and authorizes it, the App accesses the Owner's own Google account data via the Google APIs, using the following OAuth scopes:

  • .../auth/spreadsheets — read and edit the Owner's Google Sheets;
  • .../auth/documents — read and edit the Owner's Google Docs;
  • .../auth/drive — list, open, create and modify files in the Owner's Google Drive.

The App only accesses files that belong to the Owner's Google account or that have been shared with it. It does not access data of any other person.

3. How the data is used

Data is used solely to perform the actions the Owner initiates — for example, updating cells in the Owner's spreadsheets or editing the Owner's documents. The App performs no profiling, advertising, or automated decision-making.

4. Storage and sharing

  • The OAuth token is stored locally on the Owner's own computer.
  • No data is sold, rented, or shared with third parties.
  • No analytics, tracking, or third-party data processors are used.
  • Data read from Google is processed transiently on the Owner's computer and is not retained beyond what the Owner chooses to save locally.

5. Limited Use disclosure

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Data retention and revocation

The Owner can revoke the App's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access invalidates the stored token; the Owner may then delete it locally.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated. The "Last updated" date above reflects the latest revision.
