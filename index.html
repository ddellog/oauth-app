Personal Google API Utility

This is a private, single-user utility. Its owner uses it to read and edit their own Google Sheets, Google Docs and Google Drive files through the Google APIs, using OAuth authorization for their own Google account.

The tool is not a public service. It has no other users, no sign-up, and it does not process data belonging to anyone other than its owner.
